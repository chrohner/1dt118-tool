Capability against resistance

An attack succeeds when the effort the adversary can bring exceeds the effort the attack needs. Both are uncertain, so we model each as a lognormal distribution. Drag the curves or use the sliders, and watch the success probability p.

Each exercise loads the calculator with a starting situation and locks what you may not change. Targets are inequalities, so you can reach them by dragging.

Effort is measured in hours of a reference adversary (competent, design known); it is one number standing for time, money, skill and compute. All numbers are illustrations. Load-and-strength model: Rychlik & Rydén (2006), Ex. 8.1; capability against resistance as in FAIR (Freund & Jones 2014).
